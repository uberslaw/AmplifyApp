Fly through the open hollow of each coloured gate (the full art opening). Clipping the rim costs a miss — flying above or below just skips that colour. A clean pass shatters the gate into its colour. Fill all seven for a rainbow speed boost, then catch Nyan Cat. About every minute a buffalo chicken wing tumbles through — speed up or manoeuvre to grab it for a meteor shield and free miss charges.
